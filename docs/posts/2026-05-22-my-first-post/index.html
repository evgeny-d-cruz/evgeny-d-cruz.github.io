SageMath: Abstract Algebra

D12 = DihedralGroup(12)
C12 = CyclicPermutationGroup(12)

print("D12 is:", D12)
print("C12 is:", C12, "\n")

print("Is C12 normal in D12?", C12.is_normal(D12))
print("The quotient D12 / C12 is:", D12.quotient(C12))
D12 is: Dihedral group of order 24 as a permutation group
C12 is: Cyclic group of order 12 as a permutation group 

Is C12 normal in D12? True
The quotient D12 / C12 is: Permutation Group with generators [(1,2)]
